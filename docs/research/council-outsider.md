**1. Sharpest critiques (outsider eyes)**
- All four hide the ball on limits. "50 participants/month with a 30-day lockout," "5 active questions per rolling 30 days," "quiz caps at 2,000 but polls don't" — a normal person cannot predict when the tool will stop working. Mentimeter's free tier failing MID-EVENT (cap hit → lockout) is the single most hostile design I've seen. Kahoot's SKU maze (Bronze/Silver/Gold/One/One Max/One Ultra/360 Start/Standard/Plus/Max/Ultra/Spirit/Event) is genuinely unparseable.
- "Import your PowerPoint" universally means "we screenshot your slides." A first-time presenter discovers their video is a frozen image at rehearsal — or on stage. That's not import, it's a lie with a progress bar.
- Jargon walls: "Slide Sync," "Mentimote," "2-step Join," "Accuracy mode vs Classic mode vs Confidence mode," "AhaSlides GPT," "MCP server." None of this tells a presenter what happens when they click Present.
- The audience side is treated as solved but isn't: 8-digit codes that expire "about 2 days after last use," codes vs PINs vs URLs vs QR — four different join rituals. And every platform admits venue Wi-Fi kills them, then does nothing about it except Wooclap's SMS trick and a "Reconnecting…" toast.
- No undo (AhaSlides). In 2026. In an editor a presenter uses the night before.

**2. Super-system MUST do / MUST avoid**
MUST: One join ritual only — QR + one short human URL, code never expires during the event. Audience page must load in <2s on a bad phone connection, work with zero taps before answering, and auto-reconnect silently with answers queued locally. Presenter view = exactly two buttons that matter: Next, and Show/Hide results. Honest import: tell the user AT UPLOAD "your animations/videos become images" or actually preserve them — never silently degrade. Undo everywhere. A "rehearsal mode" where the presenter can fake 20 audience members and click through the whole deck the night before — none of the four offer a real test mode, which is insane for a tool used live, once, with no second chance. Big obvious "what participants see right now" preview on the presenter screen at all times.
MUST AVOID: mid-session caps of any kind (nothing may stop working while people watch); mode proliferation (one quiz, options as toggles, not five named "experiences"); AI buttons scattered through the editor — one optional "draft this for me" entry point; leaderboard music/characters on by default for adults.

**3. Trade-offs I'd accept**
Fewer question types (6–8 rock-solid beats Wooclap's 20+ — nobody outside academia knows what a Script Concordance Test is). Uglier themes for guaranteed reconnect behavior. Drop SMS fallback and LMS/API for tomorrow — spend that effort on the 300-phones-on-hotel-Wi-Fi failure mode, because that's the one that ends careers on stage.
