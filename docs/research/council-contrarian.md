THE CONTRARIAN'S VERDICT

(1) Platform critiques through the failure lens. Mentimeter — the best-funded player, running on Ably (five-nines SLA), still caps quizzes at 2,000 because realtime leaderboard fan-out is genuinely hard; their previous provider DIED at 35k connections. Kahoot needed a 2-step join specifically because bots flood open PINs. Wooclap caps at 1,000 and admits word clouds degrade at scale. AhaSlides shipped a "Reconnecting…" UI because drops were endemic at ~40 joins. Read that list again: companies with years of engineering and dedicated realtime infra all hit walls exactly where you're planning to stand tomorrow. You are proposing to out-build all four IN ONE DAY.

(2) The fatal flaws in this plan. (a) Supabase Realtime is not Ably. Default quotas: ~200–500 concurrent connections, ~100 msgs/sec on free/Pro tiers. 300 phones subscribing + a quiz question where 300 answers land in 5 seconds = throttling, silent message drops, a frozen leaderboard on stage. You cannot "stress test to 1,000" today and trust it tomorrow; you'll test synthetic clients on good network, not 300 phones on saturated venue Wi-Fi. (b) Feature list is 4 companies' roadmaps: polls, clouds, quizzes, leaderboards, Q&A+upvote, moderation, video slides, PPT/PDF/markdown import, dual export, AI generation, QR join. PPT import ALONE (rendering pptx to images serverlessly) is a day of work and every incumbent still only produces static images. This is a 6–8 week scope wearing a one-day costume. (c) No profanity filter + open word cloud + 300 adults + projector = someone types something obscene on screen. Every incumbent learned this. (d) Speed-scored quizzes punish venue Wi-Fi latency — Kahoot's documented failure mode — and you'll get "unfair!" heckling live.

MUST DO: cut to 5 slide types (MC poll, word cloud, quiz MC, Q&A upvote, static image slide); server-timestamped scoring (accuracy-mode, not speed); answers written to Postgres with presenter polling/aggregating every 1–2s instead of per-client broadcast fan-out (one channel, one aggregate message out); auto-reconnect + idempotent re-vote; profanity blocklist; a rehearsed full dry-run tonight with 50+ real phones; a printed fallback plan.

MUST AVOID: PPT import (screenshot slides to PNGs manually tonight), video-in-slide (play locally), deck export, self-paced mode, moderation queues beyond hide-one-entry, AI anything until the core survives load.

TRADE-OFFS I'D ACCEPT: ugly exports, static slides, no animations, 3-second aggregate latency, single hardcoded event — in exchange for the only metric that matters: it does not die at minute 12 in front of 300 people.
