FROM THE CORPORATE TRAINER/FACILITATOR CHAIR

(1) Sharpest critiques
- Mentimeter: the polish benchmark, but it punishes the occasional facilitator — annual-only billing, 50-participant free lockout, Q&A moderation paywalled at Pro, imports below Pro become static images. Its 2,000-quiz cap is irrelevant at 300, but its real sin for me is export: no .pptx deck export means my post-workshop report is screenshots.
- Kahoot!: reads as childish in a boardroom (characters, music, "smashers" bot attacks). Speed-based scoring punishes older/slower participants — poison for enterprise L&D fairness. Everything a trainer needs (word cloud, poll, Q&A, open-ended) is paywalled. No API, no deck export = my content is hostage.
- AhaSlides: right price, but no undo in the editor is terrifying live, exports "lack visual polish" (my client reports ARE the deliverable), and reconnect issues at ~40 joins is disqualifying at 300.
- Wooclap: best question variety, but no phone-based presenter/moderator control and the moderator interface is Pro-gated. Free tier's "5 active questions/30 days" is bait-and-switch mid-session.

(2) MUST DO
- Shareable moderator link requiring no account (steal Mentimeter's pattern): my co-facilitator moderates Q&A from a phone while I present. Profanity filter ON by default; per-slide anonymity toggle.
- One permanent QR/short-code per event (AhaSlides pattern), rendered persistently in a corner of EVERY slide — not just a join slide. Join must be: scan → type name (optional) → in, under 10 seconds, zero app, zero account. Test on 5-year-old Android phones.
- Branded everything: client logo + colors on presenter screen, audience phones, AND exports. White-label join page.
- Export as the first-class deliverable: results to xlsx (per-participant AND per-question views), branded PDF report with charts, and .pptx deck export — nobody offers that; it's my switching trigger.
- Accuracy-based quiz scoring option (Kahoot's Aug 2025 concession), leaderboard toggle, and a "presenter panic" bar: skip slide, close voting, clear responses, restart activity — one tap each.
- Phone remote for the single presenter (Mentimote pattern) — I cannot be tethered to a laptop.

MUST AVOID: participant caps that hit mid-session; any feature that dies when AI is down; per-slide join codes; undo-less editing; speed scoring as default; sound effects default-on.

(3) Trade-offs I accept
- Static-image PPT import (all four do it) IF the imported deck renders crisply and export restores fidelity. Don't burn today building animation preservation.
- Cap word-cloud rendering at ~500 visible words and batch realtime updates (250–500ms aggregation) — smoothness beats instant.
- Skip LMS/LTI, SSO, SMS fallback for v1. Do buy: a rehearsal/preview mode and a 1,000-connection load test tonight — Wooclap's degradation "near the cap" is exactly the failure I can't have on stage.
