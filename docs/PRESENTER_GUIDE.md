## 60-second setup
1. Open **your stage link** on the laptop that feeds the projector. Press **F** for fullscreen.
2. Scan the **remote QR** with your phone → the show fits in your pocket.
3. The audience scans the **QR on screen** (it's on every slide) or types the 6-letter code at the join URL. No app, no account, ~10 seconds.

## Running the show
- **Advance:** clicker/arrow keys/space on the laptop, or the big button on your phone.
- **Interactive slides** have a phase flow: **Open voting → Close → Reveal.** Polls, word clouds, scales, rankings and open-text **auto-open the moment the slide comes up** — your audience can respond immediately. **Quizzes stay manual** (the countdown starts when you open them). One contextual button on the remote walks the phases; on the laptop it's **O / C / R**. Prefer full manual control? Toggle **Auto-open: OFF** in the remote's Panic controls.
- **Quiz:** open it, let the countdown run, close, reveal (correct answer lights up), then press **L** for the leaderboard moment.
- **Q&A:** questions collect from any slide (audience has a ? button). Upvotes sort them. Moderate from the phone (approve / mark answered / archive).

## If something goes wrong (rehearsed failure scripts)
- **"It's not loading" from row 40:** "Type the six-letter code at the URL on screen." Works in any browser.
- **Your laptop dies:** reopen the stage bookmark on ANY machine — the show resumes exactly where it was. Scores, votes, questions: all safe.
- **Your phone remote dies:** the laptop keyboard does everything.
- **Venue Wi-Fi dies:** the audience is on their own data already; you can drive from your phone's data too.
- **Someone posts something ugly:** the profanity filter already blocked it; anything else is one tap (panic bar → hide) on the remote. **Lock room** stops new joins.
